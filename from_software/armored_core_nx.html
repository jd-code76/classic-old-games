Brief History of Armored Core: Nexus

Originally released on March 18th, 2004, for the PlayStation 2, Armored Core: Nexus is the first of the late-generation PS2 entries. It shipped on two discs: Evolution and Revolution. The Evolution disc continues the series' mission-based mech customization with a new story, while the Revolution disc revisits and remasters various missions from earlier games.

Nexus was the first entry in the series to adopt dual analog-controlled movement with a revised control scheme that set the template for the rest of the PS2 era. The game also includes a LAN multiplayer mode, allowing up to 4 players to link their consoles together and battle one another.
