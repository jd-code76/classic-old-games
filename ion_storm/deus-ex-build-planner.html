Weapons & Mods

Mark weapons as carried, then add mods. Stat mods (Accuracy, Magazine*, Reload, Range, Recoil) stack up to 5. Laser, Scope and Silencer install once. Damage and stats below include your skill level and Targeting aug.

*The game calls these “clips”; they are actually magazines.

Damage shown is the true effective value. According to the Deus Ex wiki, weapon-skill damage bonus is +0/+20%/+50%/+100% (untrained→master), but the in-game inventory incorrectly displays only half of it (+0/+10%/+25%/+50%). An advanced Pistol therefore really fires for 14 × 1.5 = 21, even though the inventory lists 17. Accuracy mods each add (100% − base accuracy)/10 points; each clip mod adds +1 round; each range mod adds +10% accurate range; each reload and recoil mod cuts that stat by 10%.
